1. What Earth Simulator is

Earth Simulator is a free educational tool for exploring climate projections. It visualizes data from published scientific sources (CMIP6 climate model ensembles, World Bank Open Data, and others listed in the app under "About the data") for two emissions pathways through the year 2100.

2. Not professional advice

The simulator is for education and exploration. It is not a forecast, and it is not engineering, financial, insurance, legal, medical, or emergency-planning advice. Projections are model ensemble means with real uncertainty; visual renders of events (storms, floods, fires) are stylized illustrations, not physical simulations of specific future events. Do not make safety-critical or financial decisions based on this tool.

Exported reports (including HTML downloads and print-to-PDF) carry the same disclaimers: the tool is educational, AI narrative text may contain errors, and figures require independent verification against primary sources.
