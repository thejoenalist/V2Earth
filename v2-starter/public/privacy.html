Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 5, 2026

Earth Simulator is a climate exploration tool. This page explains exactly what information the app collects, where it goes, and what never leaves your browser. We have tried to write it in plain language.

What we collect — only with your consent

When you first visit, a banner asks whether we may record your session. If you accept, we collect an anonymous "session story" — a timeline of what you explored during this visit:

  • The full text of your chat messages. Questions you type may include personal context (for example, where you live or what worries you) — anything you type is stored as-is.
  • Which countries you clicked, which map layers, years, and climate pathways you viewed, and which simulations you ran.
  • Session metadata: a randomly generated session ID, timestamps, your browser viewport size, and the page that referred you here.

This is stored in a database hosted by Supabase and is used for one purpose: understanding what people are curious about so we can improve the simulator. It is not used for advertising and is not sold or shared.

If you decline

The app works exactly the same. Nothing is recorded for your session, and the banner will ask again on your next visit. Declining is stored only in your browser's memory for the current session.

What we do NOT collect

  • No account, name, email address, or phone number — there is nothing to sign up for.
  • No precise location. We never request your device's location.
  • No advertising trackers, no analytics cookies, no fingerprinting.
  • No cross-session profile: each visit gets a new random session ID, and session IDs are not linked to each other or to you.

What stays on your device

Chat history is never saved between visits — every reload starts a fresh conversation. The only thing the app stores in your browser is a single flag recording that you accepted the telemetry banner (so we don't ask again). If you decline, not even that is stored.

Third-party processing of chat messages

To understand your questions, the text you type into the chat is sent through our server-side proxy to Anthropic (the maker of the Claude language model), which processes it to generate the simulator's response. This happens regardless of your telemetry choice — it is how the chat works — but Anthropic receives only the message text and scenario context (year, climate pathway), never a stored profile of you. See Anthropic's privacy policy.

Hosting and infrastructure

  • Netlify serves the app and, like any web host, processes your IP address in standard server logs.
  • Supabase hosts the telemetry database and the chat proxy. Our proxy uses your IP address transiently, in memory only, for rate limiting; it is not written to the telemetry database.
  • Cesium ion and NASA GIBS serve globe imagery and terrain tiles directly to your browser; those requests expose your IP address to them like any map-tile service.

Retention and deletion

Session stories are kept while we actively develop the simulator and are reviewed only by the operator. Because sessions are anonymous, we cannot identify which rows are yours after the fact; if you want a specific session removed, contact us with the approximate date and time of your visit and we will delete matching data.

Children

Earth Simulator is an educational tool suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children; do not type personal details into the chat.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the consent banner will reappear and ask again before any new collection begins.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: contact the operator via the project repository or the contact address published on the site footer.
